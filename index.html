Listening Practices

Small, deliberate ways to treat music as more than background.

1. Listen with the Body

Once a day, close the eyes and notice where the sound lands — chest, belly, jaw, feet. Track the physical response before naming the emotion.

2. Choose by Frequency

Ask what state you need (calm, focus, release) and select music that historically produces that physiology for you, not just what you like.

3. Protect Silence

Schedule periods with no input. The contrast sharpens the body’s ability to feel the difference between organised and random vibration.

4. Shared Resonance

Occasionally listen with others without talking. Notice when breathing or movement begins to synchronise. That is entrainment in real time.
